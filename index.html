who am i?

I sit where technology, story and community meet, and I make sure the work actually lands.

Ngāpuhi, Ngāti Hine, based in Te Whanganui-a-Tara. I started out behind a camera, moved into software, and now lead delivery: shaping scope, steering technical decisions and mentoring the people doing the build.

My focus is technology that serves Māori and underserved communities: data sovereignty, tech literacy and ethical AI. I'm the person who asks the awkward question early so it doesn't become an expensive problem later.
